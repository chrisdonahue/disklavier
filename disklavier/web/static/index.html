About This Database

This is an archive of MIDI piano performances primarily by Chris Donahue starting in March 2025. Performances are recorded on a Yamaha MX100II Disklavier, an acoustic upright piano equipped with digital MIDI recording and playback capabilities.

The Disklavier is connected to a Raspberry Pi that is rigged to start recording any time the piano is played. Ten seconds of inactivity will cause the recording to stop and write a MIDI file to disk. Accordingly, the recordings are candid, unpolished, and unedited. Recordings mainly consist of classical piano music, but also includes some pop covers and improvisations.

All recordings are permissively licensed under CC-BY-SA 4.0. I eventually plan to periodically release data dumps as a dataset for music information retrieval research, with a focus of capturing the process of learning classical repertoire. Contact Chris for a data dump of the entire collection.

Technical Details

  • Instrument: Yamaha MX100II Disklavier
  • Format: Standard MIDI Files (SMF)
  • Recording: High-resolution velocity and timing data
  • Processing: Sustain pedal effects integrated into note durations
  • Timezone: All timestamps in US Eastern Time
